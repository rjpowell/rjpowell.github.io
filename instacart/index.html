Competitive Intel

What are Amazon and Walmart doing with autonomous delivery?

Amazon Prime Air
Current drone cost: $63/delivery (2025 internal projection)
Standard delivery cost: ~$3.50/delivery (own network)
Third-party delivery: $4.50-5.50/delivery
Drone vs standard: 18x more expensive
Status: Testing in limited Arizona locations, paused in some areas
Constraints: Items under 5 lbs, 7 launches/hour per facility
Walmart
Milestone: 1M+ drone deliveries (2026)
Reality check: 1M is a rounding error vs 3.5B same/next-day units in Q1 2026
Coverage: 70 stores out of 4,600 US locations (1.5%)
Current cost: ~$30/delivery, targeting under $7
Payload: Max 2.5-8 lbs depending on drone
Use case: "Last-minute, urgent, convenience-driven items" only
Model: Partner-based (Wing, Zipline, DroneUp)
Key takeaway
Both are investing heavily, but scale remains tiny and economics are unproven. This is still an experiment, not a proven business.

Discovery Sprint

4-6 weeks to validate assumptions before any pilot investment

What we do
Customer: Fake door at checkout + survey
Shopper: Fake door in batch view + survey
Retailer: Portal CTA + direct conversations
Provider RFP: Cost quotes from Wing, Nuro, DroneUp
Technical review: Payload, weather, range constraints
What we learn
Demand: Do customers actually want this?
Economics: Can providers hit <$15/delivery?
Retailer appetite: Will partners opt in?
Brand risk: Will shoppers revolt?
Feasibility: What % of orders are even eligible?
Decision gate at week 6
✓ If positive: Proceed to 6-month partner pilot in Phoenix suburbs
✗ If negative: Stop here. Minimal investment lost. Revisit in 12-18 months.

What Happens Next

Present discovery findings to leadership with a clear recommendation

If Discovery Passes → Propose Pilot
Share findings with leadership and recommend:
• Where: Phoenix suburbs, 1-2 zones, Wing or Nuro
• Eligible orders: ≤10 items, ≤5 lbs, ≤3 mi, clear weather
• Customer experience: Drone option at checkout, live tracking, ETA
• Shopper experience: Dispatch to drone or deliver, focus on larger orders
• Measure: Cost, reliability, NPS, repeat usage
If Discovery Fails → Recommend Stop
Present findings and recommend not proceeding if:
✗ Provider pricing >$25/delivery
✗ Fake door click rate <2%
✗ No retailers willing to participate
✗ Shopper survey shows high backlash risk
✗ <10% of orders are drone-eligible (weight/distance)
Best case: Leadership approves pilot → Expand to more markets
Worst case: Discovery fails → Recommend stop with <$50K invested. Revisit in 12-18 months.
